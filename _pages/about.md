## Start with who I am

My full name is Haoyuan Zhang, and feel free to call me Steve. **张皓原** is my name in Chinese characters.

My hometown is a beautiful island named **[Zhoushan Islands](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%88%9F%E5%B1%B1%E7%BE%A4%E5%B2%9B)**, in the East China Sea near Shanghai. I spent all my childhood and school life there up to 18 years old.

After my undergrad in **Electrical Engineering** at [Beijing Institute of Technology](http://www.bit.edu.cn/) (with an exchange semester in CS / HCI at [PolyU, Hong Kong](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/web/en/home/index.html)), I earned my Master's in **Robotics** at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/), supervised by [Dr. Jianbo Shi](http://www.cis.upenn.edu/~jshi/) at the [GRASP Lab](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/).

Full timeline and work history live on my [resume](/cv/).

## What I am doing now

Currently, my main research and interests are about:

- traditional and modern computer vision
- a lover of 3D Vision — including Structure from Motion, Visual SLAM and AR algorithms

Feel free to check my [research project work](/talks/).
