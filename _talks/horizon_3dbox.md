**Project Owner** &middot; Vehicle and cyclist 3D bounding box detection for the auto-driving perception stack.

1. Built the detection pipeline for vehicles and cyclists, combining deep-learning models with traditional computer-vision priors to recover accurate 3D bounding boxes from monocular images.
2. Targeted the on-vehicle ADAS perception product, from data pipeline through model training and on-device deployment.
