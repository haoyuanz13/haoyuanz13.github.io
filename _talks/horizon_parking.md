**Project Owner** &middot; Camera IPM-based parking space detection that emits 3D coordinates for downstream parking planning.

1. Designed the IPM (Inverse Perspective Mapping) pipeline so a single forward camera could recover ground-plane geometry needed for parking-space localization.
2. Trained a DNN to detect parking spaces in the IPM view and output per-corner 3D coordinates ready to feed the parking module.
