**Project Owner** &middot; Vision-based, low-cost mapping and localization powering MR Spatial Anchors on PICO headsets.

1. Built the on-device mapping and re-localization stack so user-placed virtual content stays anchored across sessions.
2. Tuned the algorithm aggressively for the headset's compute and thermal envelope, keeping the cost low enough to ship to consumer devices.
